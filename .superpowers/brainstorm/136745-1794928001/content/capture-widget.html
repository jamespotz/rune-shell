Capture widget for the bar

One camera button in the notch opens a capture panel. It works with the mouse, and every option is still on a keybind. Switch the look below; both mockups follow it. Click A or B to pick a panel layout.

1 · In the bar (idle)

Placed like any widget in Settings → Menu Bar (left, center or right). Shown here on the right side of the notch.
